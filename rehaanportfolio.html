About Me

Who I Am

I'm a passionate video editor with hands-on experience since 2018, turning ideas into visually compelling stories. What started as a creative interest has grown into a professional journey, with over 3 years of freelancing experience working with diverse clients across different niches.

My goal is simple — to create edits that not only look good but also connect, engage, and leave an impact.

Experience

Over the years, I've worked on a wide range of projects including social media content, promotional videos, cinematic edits, and brand storytelling. My freelancing journey has allowed me to collaborate with clients, understand their vision, and deliver content that aligns perfectly with their goals.

I also served as the Vice President of the school tech team (Analytix) at Don Bosco School, Alaknanda, where I contributed to organizing technical events, managing creative projects, and leading a team of like-minded individuals.

Skills & Tools

I work with industry-standard tools to ensure high-quality output:

  • Adobe After Effects
  • Adobe Premiere Pro
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • DaVinci Resolve
  • Adobe Lightroom
  • Snapseed

From motion graphics and color grading to clean cuts and transitions, I bring a complete editing skillset to every project.
